SENTRA — logo files (concept 3A "Sentry ring")

Colours
  Ink     #22262B
  Accent  #3B6FE0
  Dark bg #0B0C0E
  Light bg#FAFAF9

Typeface
  Wordmark: Archivo ExtraBold (800), tracking -1 at 38px.
  The lockup SVGs use live text, so Archivo must be installed to render
  correctly. Open the SVG in Illustrator/Figma and convert the text to
  outlines for final production files.

Files
  mark/     symbol only, 48x48 grid
  lockup/   horizontal mark + wordmark, 320x64
  icon/     app icon, 512x512, corner radius 114 (iOS squircle approximation)

Usage
  Clear space: one ring radius (=20 units) on all sides.
  Minimum sizes: mark 20px, lockup 96px wide.
  Below 40px use the mono versions; the dashed inner ring may be thinned
  or dropped.
  Do not recolour the dot to anything other than the accent, white, or
  the ink mono value.
